01 / BENCHMARKING PLATFORM

GraphBench

Which graph engine fits the workload? A platform that compares NetworkX and LadybugDB with reproducible tests and correctness checks.

PythonNetworkXLadybugDBStreamlit
The engineering story +

Problem. Choosing a graph engine requires evidence beyond a speed claim.

Built. Construction and query benchmarks, throughput, memory and CPU measurement, plus CSV health checks and neighbor/reachability validation.

Learning. NetworkX was faster in the tested workloads. The result is scoped to the tested graph sizes and query patterns; engine choice should follow the workload.
